Constructed Landscapes

Film and audio documentary series

Sid Motion Gallery asked us to make a set of short films about its artists, with an audio interview series to go alongside them.

The films and interviews look at each artist, how they work and the ideas behind each piece, and they play both online and in the gallery.

Filmed at Studio 10, Victor House, SPACE Studios, London.
